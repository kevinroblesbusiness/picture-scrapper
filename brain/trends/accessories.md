# Trending Accessories, Shoes & Poses

## Jewelry
- Thin silver or gold layered chains — always works
- Thin diamond / crystal choker
- Silver star pendant necklace — baddie/kawaii
- Sculptural gold drop earrings — feminine/elegant
- Chunky stacked rings
- Oversized cuffs and chunky bracelets
- Colorful beaded / resin necklaces with gemstone pendant
- Delicate gold anklet

## Bags
- Tiny quilted mini bag with silver chain strap — black or pink
- Small beaded terracotta mini bag — warm settings
- Small baguette bag in Y2K pastels
- Chain-strap mini satin clutch
- XXL oversized structured tote (street looks)

## Sunglasses
- Bug-eyed tinted sunglasses — Gen Z Y2K
- Shield / rhinestone-studded oversized frames

## Shoes (Outdoors)
- Strappy barely-there heeled sandals — silver, nude, black, white, gold
- Strappy pointed-toe heeled sandals
- Gold heeled sandals — warm/romantic settings
- TikTok fluffy sandals — casual outdoor
- Cowboy boots — with mini skirts or denim

## Poses
- Lazy lean — back against wall, car, fence — slightly slouched, arms loose
- Leaning on car hood — both palms back, legs crossed at ankle — 8/10 proven
- Walking toward camera — candid, editorial
- One hand on hip, weight shifted — confident classic
- Hands in pockets — streetwear, laid-back
- Direct smoldering gaze — confident, sexy
- Looking off-camera — effortless, relaxed
- Head hold — hands in hair or cradling face
- Sitting on steps or curb — casual, relatable
