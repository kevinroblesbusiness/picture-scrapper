# Trending Makeup Looks

## Lips
- Glossy bubblegum pink — cute/baddie, proven for Isabella direction
- Frosted / icy pink — Y2K comeback
- Cloud lip — soft blurred edges, muted rose to deep berry — proven 8/10 (Catalina)
- Overlined glossy nude lip — crowd pleaser
- Sheer berry tint — soft and feminine

## Eyes
- Smudged smoky liner — proven 8/10 (Leah gas station)
- Thin precise upper liner only — clean and sharp
- Frosted blue icy eyeshadow with cool liner — very trending
- Holographic / opalescent / cosmic shadow
- Bold colorful graphic eyes — bright liner, bold pigment
- Light pink shimmer on lid — soft and cute

## Skin & Base
- Sharp sculpted contour + matte base — editorial
- Dewy glowing natural skin — always safe
- Breathable natural finish — "skin moving and showing through"

## Blush
- Statement blush placed high on cheekbones — very trending
- Soft pink flush — ballet core

## Full Look Combos That Work
- Smoky liner + glossy nude lip + sharp contour → Leah (8/10)
- Pink flush + cloud lip + dewy skin + feathery lashes → Catalina (8/10)
- Dewy base + thin liner + pink shimmer lid + bubblegum gloss → Isabella direction
