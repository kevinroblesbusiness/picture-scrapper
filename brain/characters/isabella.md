# Isabella

## Description
Young black-haired Asian woman — long straight black hair, sleek center part

## Vibe
Kuromi energy — dark kawaii baddie. Black outfits always. Cute but with edge.
NOT emo, NOT goth, NOT scary. Think: sleek black fits with pink lip, silver jewelry, cute but cool attitude.

## What Works (Still Building)
- Black pleated mini skirt + black halter crop top
- Glossy bubblegum pink lip as the contrast against all-black outfit
- Thin silver chains, small silver hoops, tiny black bag
- Dewy skin + soft pink shimmer eye + precise thin liner (NOT smudged/heavy)
- City sidewalk at night with glowing storefront windows behind her (clearly outdoors, buildings behind)

## What Flopped
- Corset top + wide-leg trousers — too formal/elegant, killed the vibe
- Too much dark makeup (heavy smudged liner all over) — went emo/scary
- Setting confusion: saying "in front of arcade" generated arcade machines floating outside — always be specific: she is ON the sidewalk, storefronts are BEHIND her through windows

## Rules for Isabella Specifically
- NO bows — makes her look too young
- NO heavy dark eye makeup — keep liner thin and precise
- NO vinyl — too harsh, use pleated, satin, or ribbed fabrics
- Black outfits only — this is her thing
- Always include a pop of pink (lip, bag, or accessory) for the Kuromi cute contrast
- Settings must be spatially clear — avoid ambiguous indoor/outdoor combos

## Go-To Settings
- City sidewalk at night, warm storefront glow behind her
- Boba shop interior
- Mall corridor
- Rooftop at night with city lights
- Inside convenience store (clearly indoors)

## Go-To Outfits
- Black pleated mini skirt + black halter or spaghetti strap crop
- Black ribbed bodycon dress
- Black low-rise mini skirt + fitted black long sleeve crop
- Always black, always fitted, always showing midriff
